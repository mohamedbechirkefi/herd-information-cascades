## D-002 (2026-11-24): Synthetic data only

- **Decided by:** AI proposal, within the master prompt's scope (section 10); open for the owner's review.
- **Decision:** all data are generated by a seeded simulator. No external dataset is downloaded.
- **Rationale:** the research question concerns a formal model; human laboratory data (for example the meta data set described by Weizsacker, 2008) would address human behaviour, which is outside the claims boundary, and its licence and location were not established.
- **Alternatives:** use laboratory cascade data as an external validity check (rejected for M0: unclear rights, out of scope).
- **Consequences:** no data licence risk; external validity is limited and stated as such.

## D-004 (2026-11-24): Draft protocol design choices

- **Decided by:** AI proposal; requires the owner's approval at freeze (G-FREEZE).
- **Decision:** primary metric is episode accuracy, paired against the independent baseline; imitation is operationalised as naive vote counting with own-signal tie-breaking; networks are `complete`, `window-1`, `window-3`; visibility $v \in \{1.0, 0.5\}$; ties follow the own signal in the primary analysis.
- **Rationale:** these choices keep exact Bayesian inference tractable, give an analytic anchor, and make imitation a non-degenerate rule (a pure "copy the observed majority" rule makes every agent copy the first mover under `complete` and `window-1`).
- **Alternatives:** pure copying (degenerate, rejected); general random networks (exact Bayes intractable within budget); coin-flip ties as primary (kept as sensitivity in EXP-003).
- **Consequences:** imitation and Bayesian rules coincide under `complete`; this is used as a verification check, and the comparison between them is informative only under `window-k`.
